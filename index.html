02 Maison

Cut in silence.

Huilin began above a silk merchant in Shanghai, and took its present form in Paris. The house still works slowly. Patterns are cut by hand. A collection is six ideas, not sixty.

Black, ivory, and a single note of gold. The cloth is the point. Everything else is kept quiet, so the garment can last longer than the season it arrived in.
